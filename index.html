Quick ASO Tips

  • iOS: Don't repeat words across Title, Subtitle, or Keywords. Plurals are usually handled automatically.
  • Android: Keyword density matters! Repeating important keywords reasonably across Title, Short Desc, and Long Desc is beneficial.
  • Avoid spaces in the iOS keyword bank (e.g. use game,fun not game, fun).
  • Don't include the word "App" or competitor brand names.
